# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ourcade ("we", "us", "the
app") collects when you use the iOS app, how we use it, and the choices
you have. By using Ourcade you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. Who we are

Ourcade is operated by Dominic LaMarca ("we"). You can reach us at
[domlamarca@gmail.com](mailto:domlamarca@gmail.com).

## 2. Information we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Specifically:

**Stored on your device only** (in iOS app storage, never sent to our
servers):

- Token balance, total plays, daily-challenge progress
- Personal-best scores per cabinet
- Tutorial-seen flags
- Audio and haptic preferences
- Promo codes you have redeemed

**Sent to our leaderboard service** (Supabase) so global rankings work:

- The 3-letter initials you choose inside the app (these are not your
  real name; they are an anonymous handle you select)
- Your scores per cabinet, with optional level/tap counts for compound
  scoring
- A unit label and timestamp

We do **not** collect your real name, email address, phone number,
contact list, photos, location, IP-derived precise location, advertising
identifiers, or biometric data.

**Provided to Apple, not to us**, when you make an in-app purchase:

- An anonymized purchase receipt that Apple uses to verify the purchase

We never see your Apple ID, payment method, or billing address.

## 3. How we use the information

- Display global leaderboards and compute your rank inside the app
- Award daily-challenge bonuses, personal-best bonuses, top-10 bonuses
  and top-1 bonuses based on your scores
- Persist your progress between app launches
- Process in-app token purchases through Apple's payment system

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build
a profile of you outside the app.

## 4. Children's privacy

Ourcade is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the
app and submitted scores, contact us and we will remove the records.

## 5. Third parties we use

- **Apple** — App Store distribution, in-app purchases, push
  notifications (if enabled in a future update). Apple's privacy
  practices apply: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- **Supabase** — leaderboard storage. Supabase processes the score and
  initials data described above on our behalf. Privacy details:
  <https://supabase.com/privacy>

We may add a crash-reporting service (e.g. Sentry) in the future. If we
do, this policy will be updated and the diagnostics collection will be
toggleable in the Settings screen.

## 6. Data retention

Leaderboard rows are kept indefinitely so historical rankings remain
meaningful. Because rows are tied only to your chosen 3-letter initials
and not to anything that identifies you personally, deleting your
account on the device does not require deleting them.

If you would like a specific past run removed from the public
leaderboard, email us with the cabinet name, approximate date, score,
and initials and we will remove the matching row.

## 7. Your rights

You can:

- **Delete your local account** at any time through Settings → Delete
  Account. This wipes all data on your device.
- **Request data export** by emailing us. We will send you a JSON copy
  of any rows tied to your initials within 30 days.
- **Opt out of audio, music, or haptics** through Settings.

If you live in California, the EU, the UK, or another jurisdiction with
data-protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, UK GDPR, LGPD), the same rights apply
and you can exercise them through the email above.

## 8. Security

Leaderboard data travels over HTTPS to Supabase. Local data lives in
iOS app storage protected by your device passcode. We do not run our
own servers and do not export data to other systems.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please
email us responsibly before disclosing publicly.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the date at
the top and surface a notice in the app on next launch. Your continued
use after the update constitutes acceptance.
